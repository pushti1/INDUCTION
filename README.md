# DAY 2 OF INDUCTION PROGRAM
## Morning Session: Proficiency Test

The second day of our induction program began with academic evaluations to assess our foundational knowledge in English and Mathematics.

### 📝 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM: English Proficiency Test
The day started with the English proficiency test, which aimed to evaluate our grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, and communication skills. It was a good refresher and gave insight into where we currently stand in terms of English language proficiency.

### ➕ 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Maths Proficiency Test
Following the English test, we appeared for the Mathematics proficiency test. The questions tested our understanding of core concepts from school-level Mathematics, such as algebra, calculus, and basic problem-solving. It was a balanced paper that helped us recall important fundamentals.

## ☕ 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM: Short Break 
After the tests, we had a break for approximately **one hr 30 min**. It gave us time to refresh, socialize with fellow students, and explore the campus a bit more. This break helped us relax before the next interactive session.

## 💼 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Satya Scholarship Program 2025
After the break, representatives from an external team introduced the **Satya Scholarship Program 2025**, an initiative to support students who may not be able to afford their education expenses. The session included:

- Overview of the scholarship's purpose and impact
- Eligibility criteria and required documents
- Step-by-step explanation of the application process
- A **live demonstration** where a student was invited on stage and filled out the scholarship form, which was projected on the screen for everyone to see

The program was highly informative and inspiring, as it focused on uplifting fellow students and ensuring financial support reaches those who truly need it.

## 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM: Expert Lecture by Dr. Arashdeep Singh
All **CSE** and **IT** students gathered in the **auditorium** to attend an insightful expert talk by **Dr. Arshdeep Singh** on topic
 **"Food – A Pivot Role in Student’s Overall Wellness"**

Dr. Singh emphasized the strong connection between what we eat and how we feel—physically, mentally, and emotionally. He shared how a balanced and nutritious diet directly contributes to better focus, reduced stress, and long-term health. He encouraged students to adopt mindful eating habits and avoid junk food, especially during stressful college days. He also discussed the importance of staying hydrated, getting enough sleep, and including fruits and vegetables in daily meals. One key takeaway was that students should aim to learn something new every day—not just academically but also about their own habits and lifestyle. His message was clear: **"When your body is well-fed, your mind is well-focused."**
### overall day
Day 2 was packed with both **academic evaluation** and **meaningful learning opportunities**. From proficiency tests to scholarship awareness and health guidance, the day truly reflected the balanced approach GNE takes toward student growth.

# DAY 3 OF INDUCTION PROGRAM
## 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM: Expert Lecture by Dr. Jaswinder Singh (In-charge, IAPT)
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/491628aa-c2a0-4b46-af51-a0cb4ed1e15d)
The day began with an inspiring expert lecture by **Dr. Jaswinder Singh**, the In-charge of **Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT)**. All students from **CSE and IT branches** assembled in the **auditorium** to attend this interactive session.

Dr. Singh delivered an eye-opening talk on how science is not just limited to labs or classrooms—it is a way of thinking, of observing the world with curiosity and logic. He emphasized how scientific thinking plays a crucial role in shaping innovative engineers and problem-solvers. He encouraged students to ask questions, be observant, and actively participate in events and Olympiads organized by IAPT. He also shared real life examples where students applied physics based logic to solve problems creatively. His words motivated us to look at learning beyond exams and scores and instead focus on understanding the “why” behind every concept. His message was clear: **A curious mind is the foundation of an impactful engineer.**

##  11:00 AM – 1:00 PM: Lecture on Universal Human Values by Dr. Priya Darshini
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bfadb6a-b993-4209-bee1-97816869b82c)

The next session was a deeply enriching lecture on **Universal Human Values**, conducted by **Dr. Priya Darshini Madam** in the **auditorium**. Again, all CSE and IT students were present for this session, which shifted the focus from technical knowledge to emotional and ethical development.

Dr. Priya Darshini explained how human values form the core of our identity and how they guide us in making meaningful decisions in life. She discussed essential values such as **respect, honesty, responsibility, compassion, and gratitude**, and how practicing them leads to both inner peace and societal harmony. Through simple stories and real life experiences, she made us reflect on our behavior, relationships, and the choices we make as students and future professionals. She highlighted how being a good human being is just as important as being a successful one. The session made us introspect on how we treat others and ourselves, and inspired us to adopt a more mindful and value-based approach to life. It wasn’t just a lecture  it was an experience that left a lasting impression.

## ✨1:00 PM onwards: Introduction to the Cosmic Club & Induction Activities
After the lectures, our **seniors interacted with us** and introduced us to the **Cosmic Club**, an active student-driven club in the CSE and IT department. This part of the session was also held in the auditorium and gave us a glimpse into the exciting world of extracurricular engagement.

The seniors explained that the **Cosmic Club** is a platform where students can explore their creative, technical, and leadership skills. The club conducts regular workshops, coding competitions, project exhibitions, technical fests, and fun cultural events. It is a place to **network, collaborate, innovate, and grow** beyond the classroom. They also gave us insights into the induction activities planned for the coming days, which will include team-building games, tech tasks, and interactive events designed to boost our confidence and bonding. The seniors were very friendly and encouraging, which made us feel truly welcomed and excited to be a part of the GNE family.

## ✅ Summary

Day 3 focused on **intellectual growth, ethical development, and community building**. The powerful lectures by **Dr. Jaswinder Singh** and **Dr. Priya Darshini** helped us understand how to become thoughtful engineers with values. The Cosmic Club introduction added an exciting layer to the day, reminding us that college life is also about creativity, collaboration, and contribution.
