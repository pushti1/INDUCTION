## Guest Speakers & Motivational Talks
After that, we got to hear from 
- **Mrs. Harpreet Kaur Garewal**, *Head of Applied Sciences*, she gave a really warm welcome to all the students who were present in the auditorium. She also emphasized the importance of building a strong foundation in the first year and staying consistent throughout our journey.Her words made us feel welcomed, supported, and excited to begin this new chapter.
  
- **Dr. Akshay Giddar**,*Dean Academics*, He guided us on how to manage our time effectively and stay focused on both academics and personal growth.
He also talked about the support systems available for students and encouraged us to reach out whenever we need help. His advice made us feel more confident and clear about how to move forward in our college life.

- **Harleen Kaur Garewal** a first year CSE student also shared her thoughts .She spoke about her excitement, initial nervousness, and how proud she felt to be a part of GNDEC.Her words were relatable and comforting.
  
- **Parminder Singh**, Dean of Student Welfare, He encouraged us to step out of our comfort zones, participate in college activities, and make the most of every opportunity.He also reminded us that college is not just about academics but about overall growth, friendships, and building a meaningful future
  
-  **Dr. Sehajpal Singh**, *Principal of GNDEC*, He spoke about the college’s mission, it's commitment to excellence, and how students are at the heart of everything GNDEC stands for. His message was clear with dedication, discipline, and the right mindset, we can achieve great things.Listening to him made us feel proud to be a part of such a respected institution and excited for the journey ahead.
  
-  **Shri Ashok Singla**, *Financial Advisor*, He emphasized the importance of financial literacy, smart goal-setting, and long-term vision—especially in today’s fast-changing professional world. His interaction helped the students to reflect on their own aspirations with more clarity and confidence.His words left a thoughtful impact and encouraged us to think beyond textbooks.

# OVERALL DAY
The first day of our journey at GNDEC concluded on a high note—full of inspiration, warmth, and guidance.
